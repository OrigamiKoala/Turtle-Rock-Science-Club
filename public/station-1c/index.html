When the reactor blew, it fired three beams of particles past a plate the ship had charged POSITIVE (+). The sensors traced all three.

BEAM 2went STRAIGHT THROUGH

Opposite charges pull together. Same charges push apart.

One beam is electrons (negative). One is protons (positive). One is neutrons (no charge).

Which beam is the electrons?
