## Science Sessions

**Advanced groups:** Some of the students will have sat through the lectures from last year, so they might already know a lot about some subjects. So it might be a good idea for one or two coaches per subject to prepare materials for an advanced group.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

We should give the students an idea about what the material is like for each week, so it would be great if you could create a series of questions each week, relating to what you will be teaching. For example, if I am teaching atomic theory next week, my questions might look like:

&nbsp;

What is an atom made of?

What are the different types of bonds?

	History of the Periodic Table

etc

&nbsp;

The questions should give a good idea about what the material will be covering. This way, students will be able to decide ahead of time what topics they want to take next week. Also, they will be able to figure out if they know all the material already.

&nbsp;

If they can answer all the questions, they can be part of the advanced group.

### Practice tests:

Some of the parents have asked for more structure to the team training. One of the ideas was for you all to prepare practice tests for the students. The tests will probably happen once a week or once per two weeks throughout November and December. You don't have to write the tests; you can find practice tests online.

&nbsp;

Each training meeting will be 2 hours long. You could spend the first 40-60 minutes administering the exam. After that, you can go over the questions (grade on the spot) and their solutions.

&nbsp;

This will only be for November and December. In January and February, the students will get enough practice from actual tournaments.
