3. Macro Trends in Engagement

1. Shift from Remote Buffering to In-Person Saturation:
  ⚬ In 2025–2026, 36.5% of total engagement was remote (averaging 51–54 students on Zoom each Saturday).
  ⚬ In 2026–2027, the elimination of Zoom concentrated all demand onto UCI's physical classrooms. While this reduced total nominal signups by removing low-friction remote attendees, high-interest topics now routinely hit 100% physical room capacity (Chemistry II at 71/71, Environmental Science at 40/40, Forensics at 40/40).
2. Geographic and Age Diversification:
  ⚬ Last year's main registration was 99% Turtle Rock Elementary students (110 of 111 respondents).
  ⚬ This year, TRSC successfully scaled into a regional cluster: Bonita Canyon (60 students) and University Park (56 students) now represent 53.4% of total membership.
  ⚬ The pipeline also expanded downwards into 4th grade (51 students, 23.5%), compared to 2025 when participants were almost exclusively 5th and 6th graders.
3. Subject-Driven Demand Elasticity:
  ⚬ Both years demonstrate high demand for applied and experimental sciences: Chemistry (131 signups in 2025; 100% capacity in 2026), Forensics/Crime Busters (109 in 2025; 100% capacity in 2026), and Computer Science/Build events.
  ⚬ Conversely, theoretical or heavy-memorization subjects (Epidemiology, Cell Biology, advanced Physics mechanics) experienced lower conversion (e.g., Epidemiology had 31 signups in 2025 vs. Chemistry's 131; Cell Biology + Heredity had 13 signups in 2026 vs. Forensics' 40).
4. Signup Friction and Attendance Patterns:
  ⚬ In 2025, using a single upfront Google Form created a flat, stable commitment curve (~143–147 students/week) because parents signed up for all sessions at once.
  ⚬ In 2026, weekly web signups create variable attendance driven by weekly topic appeal, ranging from 65 students during specialized topic weeks to 113+ students on multi-track weekends.

4. Methodology and Data Verification

The conclusions above were reached by directly querying and compiling the primary Google Sheets records:

1. 2025–2026 Baseline:
  ⚬ Evaluated row-level responses (149 rows) from Turtle Rock Science Club Topic Signups 25-26 to identify unique participant names (144) and emails (145).
  ⚬ Aggregated the Count tab, which calculates in-person and online attendee tallies per date.
  ⚬ Verified school and grade baselines using the TRSC Registration 2025-2026 Form Responses.
2. 2026–2027 Current State:
  ⚬ Evaluated Turtle Rock Science Club — Website Content, checking the Members tab (217 active member profiles and school/grade counts), the Newsletter tab (518 mailing list entries), and individual event reservations on the Signups tab (398 records).
  ⚬ Cross-referenced booking counts against capacity caps listed on the Events tab.
