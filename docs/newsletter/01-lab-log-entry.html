Welcome to the Turtle Rock Science Club Lab Log, our weekly newsletter! Thank you so much for joining our science community.

TRSC began in 2023 as a small group of students meeting at park picnic tables to explore science together. Since then, we have grown into an active community supporting students across Turtle Rock Elementary, University Park Elementary, and Bonita Canyon Elementary. Our coaches are high school and middle school leaders from University High School, Rancho San Joaquin Middle School, and OCSA — and our team proudly qualified for the Division B State Tournament!

Our 4 Core Goals
01.
Foster a love of science: Inspire lasting curiosity beyond rote memorization.
02.
Build community: Connect students and families who encourage and support each other.
03.
Build knowledge: Hands-on experiments, Science Olympiad training, and guest seminars.
04.
Build leadership skills: Learn and grow through teaching, collaboration, and leading.

💡 Science Tidbits: This Week’s Topics

Curious questions and facts previewing upcoming workshops:

Chemistry
Why Are Noble Gases So Aloof?

Helium, neon, and argon rarely react because their outermost electron shells are completely full (the octet rule). Since atoms react to gain or lose electrons, noble gases are already in their most stable thermodynamic state!

Astronomy
Why Do We Only Ever See One Side of the Moon?

Earth and the Moon are tidal-locked! Gravitational friction over billions of years slowed the Moon’s rotation until its orbital period matched its rotational period: exactly 27.3 days.

Computer Science
How Can 0s and 1s Create Video Games?

At the microchip level, billions of microscopic transistors act as microscopic switches. On is 1, Off is 0. By combining logic gates (AND, OR, NOT), computers perform billions of calculations every second to paint 3D worlds!

🔬 In the News: Recent Science Breakthroughs
Space Science
JWST Detects Water Vapor in Planet-Forming Disc

The James Webb Space Telescope spotted significant water vapor in the inner terrestrial planet-forming region of young star system PDS 70, proving rocky worlds can have water early on.

Biochemistry
AlphaFold 3 Expands Across All of Biology

DeepMind’s latest model predicts structures not only for proteins, but also DNA, RNA, ions, and therapeutic ligands with atomic accuracy, accelerating drug discovery worldwide.

Energy
Tandem Solar Cells Exceed 34% Efficiency

Researchers stacked crystal perovskite layers on top of standard silicon solar cells, setting a new efficiency record by capturing different spectra of sunlight simultaneously.
