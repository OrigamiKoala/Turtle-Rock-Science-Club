SUBJECT: Turtle Rock Science Club — hello, and what we do


Hi —

I'm Carl, from Turtle Rock Science Club. You signed up for updates on our
site, so I wanted to say hello properly before any newsletters start showing
up in your inbox.

The short version of who we are: we're a group of students, parents, and
mentors in Irvine. Our coaches are middle and high school students from
University High, Rancho San Joaquin, and OCSA — students teaching students,
which works better than you'd think. We run hands-on science sessions on
Saturday evenings, and some students go on to compete in Science Olympiad.

Sessions are Saturdays, 7:00-8:30 PM, at UCI's Paul Merage School of
Business, room SB2-117. Three rooms run at once, each on a different subject,
and students pick the one they want to explore.

Everything else — upcoming sessions, sign-ups, and write-ups from past
meetings — is at trscienceclub.org.

If you have questions, just reply to this. It reaches a real inbox.

Carl
Turtle Rock Science Club
